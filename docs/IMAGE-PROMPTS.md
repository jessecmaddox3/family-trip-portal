## Project hero

Create a polished, playful editorial illustration for the open-source project 'Family Trip Portal'. Wide 2:1 landscape composition. A warm sand-colored travel scrapbook spread seen in gentle isometric perspective: a small imaginary seaside cottage, curling deep navy and sea-glass waves, a coral-colored suitcase, a paper calendar, a shared dinner table with dishes, a tide chart drawn as a simple wave on a card, and a stack of vacation snapshots showing only scenery, no identifiable people. Crisp handmade paper-cut texture and fine ink outlines, tasteful sophisticated illustration, cozy and useful rather than corporate. Palette warm cream, deep ocean navy, muted sea-glass green, terracotta coral, and golden sand. Beautiful readable typography at top left with exactly 'Family Trip Portal' and below in smaller type exactly 'One place for the whole trip.' No other text, no logos, no addresses, no personal information. The drawing should feel like a fun mini product sell-page while remaining a single cohesive illustration. All locations and objects entirely invented.
